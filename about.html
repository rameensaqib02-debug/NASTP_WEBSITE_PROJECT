Who We Are

The Space Sustainability Centre (SSC) is a specialized initiative under NASTP Rawalpindi, committed to space technology excellence, environmental monitoring, and national resilience. SSC integrates modern tools like satellite imagery, remote sensing, and drone-based mapping to support planning, climate action, urban analytics, and disaster response.

OUR OBJECTIVES

LEADING SPACE TECHNOLOGY IN PAKISTAN

● Strengthen space sustainability initiatives.
● Empower research in geospatial sciences.
● Bridge academia, startups, and policy through training & data services.
● Support national priorities through space-based solutions.

CORE FUNCTIONS

TECHNOLOGY, TRAINING & IMPACT

SSC provides high-quality GIS mapping, terrain modeling, drone surveys, satellite data processing, and workshops. It offers hands-on labs, R&D collaboration opportunities, and consultative services to boost technological independence.
